# Design notes

Studied for structure only. Nothing below was copied: no sentences, photos, layouts, or code.

Sites reviewed:

1. [Caliber](https://www.caliber.com/) puts a phone-level next step in the first screen.
2. [Gerber Collision & Glass](https://www.gerbercollision.com/) treats the phone number as the main action.
3. [Crash Champions](https://www.crashchampions.com/) lists the repair from drop-off to hand-back.
4. [Fix Auto](https://www.fixauto.com/en/) uses a short “why us” block and separate service cards.
5. [Craftsman Collision](https://craftsmancollision.com/services/) (Canada) starts the visit with an estimate.
6. [Craftsman Collision USA](https://www.craftsmancollisionusa.com/) talks through the repair instead of hiding it.
7. [Boyd Autobody](https://www.boydautobody.com/) keeps the offer to collision repair, not a long catalog.
8. [CARSTAR](https://www.carstar.ca/) leads a multi-shop brand with a locator.
9. [Kirmac](https://kirmac.com/) puts a street address and phone on the local page.
10. [BMW Certified Collision Repair Centres](https://bmwccrc.ca/en/Kirmac) shows one precise credential, not a wall of badges.
11. [Reborn Autobody](https://rebornautobody.ca/) uses a dark page and a strong phone line for a premium shop.
12. [Abra](https://www.abraauto.com/) splits work into a few named services.

How those ideas were changed:

- The usual first screen is a stock photo of a car with a slogan over it. This page is a black “scan bay”: an original line drawing of a generic body shell, a slow gold laser line, panel-gap rules, a faint carbon weave, and a gloss sweep. All of that is CSS and SVG drawn for this site.
- Call and Get an estimate are the two actions. Call uses the shop phone. Get an estimate opens an email to regalltd@shaw.ca. There is no copied claim form.
- The repair rail has the six stations named for this shop: estimate, parts, repair, paint, quality check, delivery. It is not another shop’s intake, scan, or calibration list.
- Services are only collision repair, auto body, and paint.
- Why us uses only what this shop can say: I-CAR Gold Class, one Richmond shop for those three jobs, and a repair path the visitor can see. No warranty, no years-in-business, no OEM brand list.
- Credentials show the supplied I-CAR Gold Class logo only. The ICBC slot stays in a comment until written approval.
- Location is one address, set like a work order, with a link to a map. There is no embedded map frame and no store finder.
- Before and after is an original slider: a range control clips two placeholder frames. Shop photos can be dropped in later. No photos were taken from other sites.

Motion (the laser and the gloss sweep) is turned off when the visitor has asked the system to reduce animation.
